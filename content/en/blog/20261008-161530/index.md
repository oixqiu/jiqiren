Actually, what interests me most right now is the robotics field. For this, I even set up a robotics website. Why do I say I'm most interested in robots? Because robotics will have a dividend for 10 to 50 years in the future. For any industry we're working in now, the dividends are already very short. Large language models—we play with robots and do AI well, but in robotics, we're only deep experiencers. At the same time, we can probably earn twice as much by distributing robots and joining the industry. So, from this perspective, I'm very optimistic about robotics. This is my recent core point: to make robots excellent, to the extreme. All my thoughts on robots are shared with my robot outlets.
