Today, I heard some discussions about humanoid robots, and I strongly agree with their viewpoints. I think the application scenarios for humanoid robots have not yet been fully identified. Many current needs can be met with fixed robotic arms. But what scenarios humanoid robots are suitable for, what market and space these scenarios are, none of this remains uncertain.

But I still firmly believe that humanoid robots will have their own ten-billion, or even greater opportunities. It's just that we haven't discovered this opportunity yet. Just because it hasn't been discovered doesn't mean it doesn't exist.

Just like the iPhone back in the day—before it appeared, did we ever realize it could truly awaken an era, making anyone make their phone their primary feature? So what about the future? Is it possible for everyone to have a humanoid robot? It is your avatar, relying on your own perception for mutual existence. Perhaps this is what we call a "shadow worker," or even a "shadow experience world," or "digital immortality."

That is, we can fully use brain-computer interfaces, connect with them anytime, even fully interconnect, and experience another world. Then, when the brain-computer interface is connected, we can combine corresponding cognition and perception. I believe such an era will surely come.

By then, it might not be humanoid; it could be humanoid, or in other forms, or even bacterial. We look forward to such a world arriving soon.
