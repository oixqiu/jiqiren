The most valuable thing in your body may be being "harvested"

Have you ever thought that the most valuable thing in your life might not be a house, not savings, or even your life, but that invisible, intangible, and intangible thing inside your body—your soul.

Don't rush to scroll away. Let me ask you a question: If one day robots can completely replace your labor, and artificial intelligence can completely replace your thinking, what will you have left?

I thought about this question for a long time. I forced myself to study and think every day in the three fields of artificial intelligence, robotics, and philosophy. Why these three? Because they promote and replace each other. Artificial intelligence replaces human thinking, robots replace human labor, and philosophy is the final destination of all problems that science cannot explain.

Have you noticed something very strange? We always say the soul is in the brain, but the old tradition has always believed the soul is in the heart. Science still cannot prove the existence of the soul, nor can it prove it does not exist. Whether something can be disproven determines whether it is science. Recently, I came across some things about Akashic interpretation; some parts can be disproven, and some parts seem to be proven. But that kind of confirmation is like Buddhism holding up a finger, letting you interpret it.

What does this indicate? It shows that the stronger our research capabilities, the more unknowns there are.

I increasingly feel that perhaps only when we truly create robots like humans will we understand what a soul is. And until then, please remember one thing: in this universe, the most important resources may no longer be steel, oil, or chips, but a vibrant, pure soul.

To put it in a chilling way—maybe we really are being kept captive by higher-dimensional beings. And what they need is simply us pure souls.

If that's really the case, then the only thing you can truly present and possess infinite value may be that soul that even you can't explain or understand, yet remains as pure as ever.

So, protect it. Don't let it get dirty by this noisy world.
