Nowadays, people often forget to drink water, which is actually a very painful issue. If there really were a robot, it would tell you to drink water when the time comes. This could really solve a big problem. It's like some leaders, who have people attending to them. When it's time to drink water or what needs to be done, there are corresponding staff to handle it. In the future, we may all have such a little robot, which will bring water over and tell you when it's time to drink. Whether it's wheeled or not doesn't matter. At least it can maintain balance, and at least it doesn't need to use an open water delivery, even if it's bottled. When you tell it a task and it reminds you when it's not, it's not as annoying as the ding-ding ringing on our phones now. Instead, it comes straight up to you, appears right in front of you, and tells you what to do. I think once we have this time limit, our efficiency will be double what it is now.
