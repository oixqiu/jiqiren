# AI Laser Mosquito Killer Robot (Grassland Edition)

The AI Laser Mosquito Killer Robot is a high-profile biomimetic quadruped smart disinfection device designed for open outdoor scenes such as courtyards, community lawns, balcony flower beds, outdoor turf and family gardens. Unlike low-profile working robots, its moderately tall, slender body works from an elevated downward-viewing position to deliver safe, precise and fully automatic mosquito control, eliminating the common problems of traditional devices: harm to people, limited coverage and incomplete elimination. It is a new-generation safe smart outdoor mosquito killer.

## Biomimetic Quadruped Walking Structure

The robot uses a lightweight biomimetic quadruped walking structure. Four self-adaptive mechanical legs support the tall body with stable, highly flexible movement, easily adapting to lawns, mud, stone paths and flower-bed corners.

- Autonomously moves through greenery, grass and gaps between landscape plants for full-area patrol and elimination
- Quadruped body with self-adaptive leveling and intelligent obstacle avoidance
- Automatically avoids rocks, plants and outdoor obstacles, flexibly adjusting the walking path
- Fully autonomous patrol without manual supervision; suited to all-weather outdoor operation

## Overhead Downward-View Laser Design

The core working system adopts an overhead downward-view laser elimination design as a dedicated safety architecture. An integrated vision and laser elimination module is mounted on top of the body, using the height advantage of the tall chassis to create a top-down view and elimination path.

- Built-in program locks the laser emission angle, strictly limiting it to downward firing only
- Eliminates all dangerous angles such as level sweeping or upward emission
- Laser always points vertically or at a small angle toward the ground, grass and low vegetation where mosquitoes gather
- Avoids sweeping people or pets at the source, solving the safety concern of outdoor laser operation
- Suited for courtyards and outdoor leisure areas where people are active

## AI Vision Perception System

The module carries a HD AI vision perception system that scans grass, vegetation and low-altitude areas in real time, accurately recognizing flying, resting and hidden mosquitoes, moths and other harmful insects while distinguishing people, pets and beneficial insects for precise targeting, eliminating ineffective or misjudged elimination.

- Vision system works in real-time coordination with the laser elimination module
- Integrated workflow of downward-view recognition, point locking and vertical firing elimination
- Precisely burns mosquitoes hidden in grass, leaf surfaces and ground crevices
- Full-area refined mosquito elimination

## Smart Audio Announcement System

A built-in HD smart audio system with a safety announcement function serves as one of the device's core safety configurations. When the device starts operating or enters the disinfection state, the audio automatically plays a safety notice in a loop, reminding nearby people in real time that laser mosquito-killing is in progress, to stay away from and not touch the device.

- Supports adaptive volume adjustment for noisy outdoor environments
- Clear and effective alerts that protect the outdoor activity safety of pedestrians, children and pets

## Intelligent Full-Area Patrol System

The device is equipped with an intelligent full-area patrol system supporting custom working zones, scheduled start/stop, patrol elimination cycles and blind-spot rechecks, memorizing mosquito hotspots and reinforcing elimination frequency there to sustainably suppress outdoor mosquito breeding.

With no chemicals, no smoke and no odor, the robot uses pure physical laser elimination — green, environmentally friendly and harmless. Combined with the angle-locked laser mode and the smart voice alert system, it delivers both efficient mosquito control and all-round safety, perfectly fitting family courtyards, villa outdoors and small community green areas.
