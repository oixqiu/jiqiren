# AI Item-Finding Robot

The AI Item-Finding Robot is a whole-home smart device based on a mobile base with AI vision recognition. Its overall shape and movement are similar to a home robot vacuum: a low, rounded one-piece body with no sharp edges that fits most indoor scenes such as homes and offices, freely moving under desks, sofas and beds to reach low, narrow spaces. It delivers corner-to-corner mobile patrol and item searching, perfectly solving the everyday pain points of lost small items and misplaced belongings.

## Mobile Base and Chassis

The bottom uses the proven robot vacuum movement solution with anti-slip swivel wheels, drive wheels and anti-collision sensing modules, supporting autonomous obstacle avoidance, whole-home route planning, fixed-point patrol, in-place turning and quiet, stable movement.

- Long battery life for extended autonomous whole-home searching without manual control
- Balanced stability and scene adaptability across wood floors, tiles and carpets
- Compact and slim body that maximizes space access, covering hidden areas hard to inspect manually

## Rising Panoramic AI Vision Module

The core differentiator is the rising panoramic AI vision module on top of the body — a fundamental departure from the fixed sensing structures of ordinary robot vacuums and the key hardware for precise item finding.

- Independent motorized lift with multiple height levels for autonomous adjustment
- Adjusts to the search scene, item height and obstructions, avoiding furniture and clutter
- Expands field-of-view coverage, combining close-probe inspection of low spaces with elevated scanning of high areas

## Dual-Axis Free Rotation

The vision module rotates freely on two axes for blind-spot-free visual coverage of the whole scene.

- **Horizontal 360°**: continuous rotation without the body turning, enabling ring scanning of the entire room and quick inspection of all items and surroundings
- **Vertical 180°**: up-and-down tilt, pointing straight down to the floor or level with desks, counters and low shelves
- Precisely covers items scattered on the floor, placed on surfaces and tucked in low compartments
- Solves the limited field of view and high-low blind spots of ordinary devices

## AI Vision Recognition and Precise Location

With a HD camera and proprietary AI vision recognition algorithms, the robot accurately identifies high-frequency lost small items such as keys, earphones, remote controls, cards, jewelry and stationery.

- Supports custom item registration, targeted search, full-area blind search, item location memory and track replay
- Autonomously plans search paths, scanning the environment from multiple angles with the rising, rotating lens and comparing item features in real time
- Once a target item is recognized, locks its position and locates it immediately
- Reports the item coordinates, live images and search track to the mobile app, and can navigate to the target location

## Smart Sensing and Protection

The device carries a smart sensing protection system that automatically recognizes people, pets and obstacles during movement and lens rotation, pausing actions in real time and avoiding collisions for both safety and a smart experience.

Compared with the single cleaning function of traditional robot vacuums, this AI Item-Finding Robot centers on panoramic dynamic vision and focuses on whole-home item finding — a brand-new home smart service robot built for everyday item retrieval needs.
