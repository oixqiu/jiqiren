# Artificial intelligence object-finding robots

The AI object search robot is a whole-house intelligent locating device based on a mobile chassis + AI visual recognition. Its overall design and movement are highly similar to household robot vacuums, featuring a low, rounded integrated design with no sharp edges, suitable for most indoor scenarios such as homes and offices. It can freely move between low and narrow spaces such as under desks, sofas, and beds, enabling seamless mobile inspection and item searching throughout the house, perfectly solving daily pain points like lost small items or items left scattered and hard to find.

## Moving the chassis and fuselage

The bottom of the body continues the mature robot vacuum movement solution, equipped with anti-slip universal wheels, drive wheels, and anti-collision sensing modules, supporting autonomous obstacle avoidance, whole-house route planning, fixed-point cruise, in-place steering, and silent smooth movement.

- Long-lasting battery life, allowing you to autonomously explore the whole house for extended periods without manual operation
- Balancing mobility stability and scene adaptability, it is suitable for various floor environments such as wooden floors, tiles, and carpets
- The body is overall compact and slim, with a low-profile design that maximizes space limitations, covering hidden areas that are difficult for humans to detect

## Adjustable panoramic AI visual camera module

The core differentiating feature is the adjustable panoramic AI visual camera module mounted on the top of the body, which completely sets it apart from the fixed sensor structure of ordinary robot vacuums and serves as the core hardware for precise object finding.

- Equipped with an independent electric lifting bracket, supporting multi-level height adjustment
- Automatically raises and lowers based on search scenarios, item height, and occlusion to avoid furniture and clutter obstruction
- Enhances field of view coverage, balancing low-profile space snug detection and high-altitude scanning needs

## Two-dimensional freely rotating viewing angle

The camera module features dual-dimensional free rotation capability, achieving blind-spot visual coverage across all scenes.

- **Horizontal 360°**: Continuous rotation without blind spots, completing a full house environmental circular scanning without the need to rotate the device
- **Vertical 180°**: Vertical tilt adjustment, allowing the lens to vertically press down against the floor and face up at tabletops, countertops, and low shelves
- Accurately covers objects of different heights, such as scattered items on the ground, items placed on tables, and low-level layered items
- Completely solves the problems of limited visibility and many blind spots in ordinary equipment

## AI visual recognition and precise positioning

Relying on high-definition cameras combined with self-developed AI visual recognition algorithms, the robot can accurately identify various frequently lost small items such as keys, headphones, remote controls, cards, jewelry, and stationery.

- Supports core functions such as custom item entry, fixed-point location, global blind search, item location memory, and trajectory playback
- Independently plan search paths, using lift + panoramic rotating camera to scan the environment from multiple angles and compare item features in real time
- Once a target item is identified, it immediately locks onto its location and pinpoints it precisely
- Provides feedback on item coordinates, real-life footage, and search trajectories via mobile app, supporting nearby navigation to target locations

## Intelligent induction protection system

The device is equipped with an intelligent sensing protection system that automatically detects people, pets, and obstacles during movement and camera rotation, pausing actions in real time to avoid collisions, balancing safety and intelligent experience.

Compared to traditional robot vacuums with only a single cleaning function, this AI find-and-find robot centers on panoramic dynamic vision, focusing on whole-house intelligent locating scenarios. It is a brand-new smart home service robot designed specifically for daily item searching needs.
