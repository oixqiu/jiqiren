# AI Elevated Laser Mosquito Killer Robot

The AI Elevated Laser Mosquito Killer Robot is a high-end outdoor smart device purpose-built for eliminating low-altitude flying mosquitoes, complementing the ground-based downward-view mosquito killer with scenario coverage. Equipped with a biomimetic quadruped mobile chassis and a mast that extends to adult height, the device focuses on mosquitoes hovering in mid-air and low-altitude airspace. Its dedicated upward-view recognition and elevated laser elimination technology solve the key limitation of conventional devices that can only clear mosquitoes in ground grass but cannot reach flying mosquitoes in the air. With its purpose-built safety architecture, the robot fully prevents laser from harming people or pets, making it ideal for courtyards, terraces, outdoor leisure areas and open spaces in parks.

## Biomimetic Quadruped Mobile Chassis

The robot uses a stable biomimetic quadruped walking structure. Four thickened self-adaptive mechanical legs provide terrain-adaptive leveling, cushioned walking and intelligent obstacle avoidance, adapting to lawns, mud, stone-paved paths and slightly uneven outdoor ground.

- Smooth, quiet movement with low vibration
- Never crushes greenery or damages the garden landscape while walking
- Supports fully automatic full-area patrol, fixed-point guard duty and zone-based patrol cycles
- No manual control or supervision required; supports all-weather outdoor routine operation

## Rising Mast for Elevated Operation

The signature configuration is a rising, retractable waist mast that distinguishes this robot from ordinary low-profile working robots. The mast autonomously extends to raise the top working module to adult height, forming an elevated working position.

- The elevated design lifts the elimination field of view and laser working range into the low-altitude airspace above people
- Physically prevents laser from sweeping across people or pets on the ground
- Improves outdoor operation safety at the source and suits open outdoor scenes where people are active

## Integrated Smart Elimination Module

The top of the mast integrates an all-in-one smart elimination module with multiple sensing and elimination hardware units, including front-facing and upward-facing HD AI cameras for full low-altitude coverage.

- Front view: anticipates the surroundings, identifies people and obstacles, and avoids operational risks
- Upward view: exclusively locks onto the airspace above, precisely capturing hovering, flying and circling mosquitoes, moths and other harmful insects
- Intelligently distinguishes people, birds, beneficial insects and mosquito targets for precise targeting
- Prevents misidentification and erroneous elimination, ensuring precision and ecological safety

## Directional Laser Elimination Unit

The module carries multiple directional laser elimination units, a dedicated high-altitude mosquito-killing configuration. Laser angles are double-locked by hardware and software, allowing upward operation only, with no downward sweeping function, completely eliminating the risk of harming people on the ground.

- Covers a large fan-shaped airspace of up to 120 degrees upward
- Multiple laser beams work in coordination with AI vision to track flying mosquitoes in real time
- Dynamically scans, locks onto and precisely burns mosquitoes across a wide airspace
- Efficiently clears mosquitoes hovering and lingering at low altitude, filling the high-altitude blind spot of ground devices
