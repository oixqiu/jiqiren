# The high-altitude AI laser mosquito control robot

The AI high-altitude laser mosquito control robot is a high-end outdoor smart device designed to eliminate low-altitude flying mosquitoes, complementing the ground-level disinfection robot in various scenarios. The device uses a bionic quadrupedal mobile chassis, paired with an ultra-long adjustable waist telescopic rod. Its working height can be raised to the height of adults, focusing on floating mosquito groups flying in the air and at low altitude. Through exclusive upward-view recognition and high-altitude laser disinfection technology, it completely solves the problem of ordinary mosquito control devices that can only clear grass and mosquitoes on the ground but cannot eliminate flying mosquitoes in the air. Relying on a dedicated safety structure design, it prevents laser accidental injuries to humans and pets throughout the process, adapting to scenarios such as courtyards, terraces, outdoor leisure areas, and park open spaces.

## Bionic quadruped mobile chassis

The robot's body adopts a stable bionic quadruped walking structure, with four thickened adaptive mechanical legs capable of terrain-adaptive leveling, shock-absorbing movement, and intelligent obstacle avoidance. It can adapt to various complex terrains such as lawns, dirt surfaces, stone pavements, and slight undulating outdoor surfaces.

- Smooth body movement, quiet operation, and low vibration
- Do not crush the greenery or damage the courtyard landscape while walking
- Supports fully automatic full-area cruise, fixed-point disinfection, and regional cyclic inspections
- No need for manual control or supervision, supporting routine outdoor operations around the clock

## Vertical posts capable of raising and lowering work at heights

The core flagship feature of the equipment is a waist-mounted retractable raised vertical post. Unlike conventional low-altitude work robots, the vertical rod can be raised and extended autonomously, raising the top work module to the standard height of adults to create a high-altitude work position.

- High-position design is removed from low-lying areas of the ground, with the disinfection field and laser operation focusing on low-altitude airspace above the human body
- Physically avoid laser attacks on humans and pets moving on the ground
- Fundamentally enhances outdoor work safety, adapting to open outdoor scenarios where people are active

## Integrated intelligent sterilization module

The top of the pole integrates an integrated intelligent sterilization module, equipped with multiple sensing and sterilization hardware, and is equipped with dual-angle high-definition AI cameras facing forward and upward, achieving full-area low-altitude field coverage.

- Forward-facing view: anticipates the surrounding environment in advance, identifies people and obstacles, and avoids operational risks
- Upward view: Exclusive lock-on airspace for precise capture of hovering, flying, and circling mosquitoes, moths, and other harmful insects
- Intelligently distinguishes targets for humans, birds, beneficial insects, and mosquitoes, precisely screening targets for disinfection
- Eliminate misidentification and disinfection, ensuring operational accuracy and ecological safety

## Directional laser disinfection device

The module is equipped with multiple directional laser disinfection devices, providing a customized solution for high-altitude mosquito control. The laser angle is locked by both system hardware and programming, supporting only upward airspace operation without downward sweep function, completely avoiding the risk of injury to ground personnel.

- Supports upward 120-degree super fan-shaped airspace coverage
- Multiple laser beams work together, combined with AI vision for real-time tracking and locking onto flying mosquitoes
- Dynamic scanning of large-area airspace, fixed-point locking, and precise burn and disinfection
- Efficiently clears low-altitude hovering and lingering mosquitoes, filling the high-altitude disinfection blind spots of ground mosquito control equipment

## Dual safety protection system

The device continues the security alert configuration, building a dual security protection of "voice alerts + physical structure":

- The device has a built-in high-definition smart audio system that automatically loops safety prompt voice messages when starting high-altitude disinfection operations
- Remind nearby personnel that equipment is performing high-altitude laser mosquito control operations; do not stay close to or touch the equipment
- Equipped with an intelligent area limitation system that allows customizable work areas and automatically avoids crowded areas
