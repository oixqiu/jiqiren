# Artificial intelligence laser weeding robots

The AI laser weeding robot is a brand-new lightweight quadruped intelligent outdoor work robot, specifically designed for precise weeding in courtyards, lawns, vegetable gardens, flower beds, small farmlands, and other scenarios. The entire machine abandons the bulky structure of traditional wheeled and tracked weeding equipment, adopting a bionic quadruped walking body. The body is compact, lightweight, highly passable, requires no soil turning, no pesticides, and no physical crushing. Based on AI visual recognition + precise laser melting as its core working principle, it achieves fully automated, zero-pollution, and refined intelligent weeding, completely replacing manual weeding, chemical weeding, and mechanical mowing methods.

## Bionic quadruped body structure

The main body adopts a bionic four-legged design, with four mechanical legs supporting independent adaptive adjustment, flexibly adapting to uneven grasslands, gently undulating muddy ground, stone slab gaps, flower bed corners, and other complex terrains.

- Compared to wheeled robots, the four-legged structure does not cause grass jams or ground sinking
- Walk gently, do not run over greenery, do not trample lawn vegetation
- Precisely weaves through flower beds and gaps between vegetable seedlings to maximize protection of beneficial crops and landscape vegetation
- Built-in intelligent obstacle avoidance and posture balance system automatically avoids stones, dead branches, and small debris
- Fully autonomous operation, no manual operation required

## Electric lifting precision telescopic rod

At the center of the robot's waist is a precision telescopic rod that can be lifted and lifted electrically, providing highly adaptive adjustment for core operational components.

- Supports multi-level precise lifting and lowering, automatically adjusting work height based on weed height, crop layer height, and terrain elevation differences
- Can remove dwarf weeds close to the ground and raise the height to address tall weeds
- Avoid blocking from flowers, vegetable leaves, and other beneficial plants above
- Adapts to weed removal needs at different growth stages, with comprehensive coverage and extremely high fault tolerance

## Integrated intelligent operation module

The top of the lifting rod integrates an integrated intelligent operation module with an ultra-simple and compact structure, equipped with a dual-core component of a high-definition intelligent surveillance camera + precision laser emitter. The module structure is stable and contains no redundant parts.

- High-definition cameras feature full-coverage HD imaging and AI intelligent recognition capabilities
- Accurately distinguishes weeds, vegetables, flowers, and lawn greenery
- Independently identify weed species, locations, and root system root points
- Precisely targets the core area of weed roots, preventing misjudgment and harming beneficial vegetation

## Laser precision root elimination

The laser emitter works in sync with the vision system, relying on AI for precise positioning, focusing on weed roots to emit high-energy, precise lasers. Using high-temperature melting, it precisely burns and removes weed roots, completely eradicating weeds from the roots and preventing repeated weed growth.

- Completely contactless, no soil damage, no pesticide residues, no dust or noise
- Cleaner, more environmentally friendly, and more refined compared to traditional weeding methods
- Precisely and precisely removes weeds in gap areas, weeds between dense greenery, and small grasses that are difficult to clean manually, all of which are difficult to clean manually

The entire machine is equipped with an autonomous path planning system, supporting full-area automatic cruising, precise targeted weeding, area delimitation operations, and repeated area re-inspection. It can remember weeding areas and weed points, regularly revisit and clear new weeds, and achieve long-term intelligent maintenance of courtyard and vegetable garden lawns.

The overall body is minimalist with technology, quadrupedal mobility is flexible and adaptable to complex outdoor scenarios, and its adaptive lifting operation combined with AI laser precise root removal makes it a new generation of zero-pollution, finely detailed, fully automatic outdoor smart weeding device.
