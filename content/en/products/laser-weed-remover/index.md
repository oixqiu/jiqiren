# AI Laser Weeding Robot

The AI Laser Weeding Robot is a brand-new lightweight quadruped smart outdoor working robot designed for precise weeding in courtyards, lawns, vegetable gardens, flower beds and small farms. Abandoning the heavy structures of traditional wheeled and tracked weeding machines, it uses a biomimetic quadruped body that is compact, light and highly passable. With no tilling, no chemicals and no physical crushing, it works on AI vision recognition plus precise laser melting to deliver fully automatic, zero-pollution, refined intelligent weeding, fully replacing manual pulling, chemical weeding and mechanical mowing.

## Biomimetic Quadruped Body

The main body uses a biomimetic quadruped structure. Four mechanical legs support independent self-adaptive adjustment, flexibly handling uneven grass, slightly undulating mud, stone crevices and flower-bed corners.

- Compared with wheeled robots, the quadruped structure has no grass-jamming or sinking problems
- Gentle walking that never crushes greenery or tramples lawn vegetation
- Precisely moves between flower clusters and vegetable seedlings, maximizing protection of beneficial crops and landscape plants
- Built-in intelligent obstacle avoidance and posture balancing system that automatically avoids rocks, dead branches and small obstacles
- Fully autonomous operation without manual control

## Motorized Lifting Precision Mast

A motorized precision lifting mast is mounted at the center of the robot's waist, providing height-adaptive adjustment for the core working module.

- Supports multiple precise lift levels, automatically adjusting the working height to weed height, crop canopy height and terrain changes
- Clears low ground-hugging weeds while also rising to handle taller weeds
- Avoids obstruction by beneficial plants such as flowers and vegetable leaves above
- Covers weeds at different growth stages comprehensively with high tolerance

## Integrated Smart Working Module

The top of the mast integrates an all-in-one smart working module that is extremely simple and compact, carrying two core components — a HD smart vision camera and a precision laser emitter — with a stable structure and no redundant parts.

- HD camera with full-area high-definition imaging and AI recognition
- Precisely distinguishes weeds from vegetables, flowers and lawn plants
- Autonomously identifies weed species, position and root points
- Locks onto the core root area of weeds, preventing misjudgment and damage to beneficial plants

## Precision Laser Root Removal

The laser emitter works in synchronization with the vision system. Guided by AI precise positioning, it fires high-energy precision laser at the weed root, using high-temperature melting to burn and clear the root system, eliminating weeds thoroughly at the root and preventing regrowth.

- Fully contactless: no soil disturbance, no pesticide residue, no dust and no noise
- Cleaner, more environmentally friendly and more refined than traditional weeding
- Precisely removes hard-to-reach weeds in crevices, dense vegetation gaps and tiny ground-hugging weeds that are difficult for manual work

## Autonomous Path Planning System

The robot carries an autonomous path planning system supporting full-area automatic patrol, fixed-point precise weeding, zoned operation and repeated-area rechecks, memorizing weeding zones and weed points and returning periodically to clear new growth for long-term intelligent maintenance of courtyards and vegetable gardens.

With its minimalist tech design, a quadruped body that flexibly adapts to complex outdoor scenes, height-adaptive operation and AI laser root removal, it is a new-generation zero-pollution, refined, fully automatic outdoor smart weeding device.
